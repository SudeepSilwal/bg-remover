Last updated: September 9, 2026

RemoveBG is designed to minimize the information we collect. You can use the image background removal tool without creating an account.

When you upload an image, the image is temporarily transferred to our backend service so that the background can be removed.

We do not require your name, phone number, or account information to use the basic background removal tool.
