Remove backgrounds from images online for free with RemoveBG. Upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP image and automatically remove its background. Download the result as a transparent PNG for product photos, profile pictures, social media graphics, websites, presentations, and other designs.

What is a background remover?

A background remover is an image-editing tool that detects the main subject of a photograph and removes the surrounding background. The result can be saved with transparency, making it useful for product photos, profile images, graphics, presentations, online stores, and social media content.

How do I remove a background from an image?

Upload your image to RemoveBG, select Remove Background, wait for processing to finish, and download the resulting transparent PNG. No manual selection or photo-editing software is required.

Can I remove a JPG background?

Yes. JPG images can be uploaded and processed. Because JPG does not support transparency, the processed result is provided as a PNG file so the removed area can remain transparent.

Can I remove a PNG background?

Yes. PNG images are supported. The resulting image can be downloaded as a transparent PNG.

Can I remove a WebP background?

Yes. RemoveBG supports WebP uploads in addition to JPG and PNG.

What can I use a transparent image for?

Transparent images can be useful for product listings, advertisements, social media graphics, websites, presentations, logos, profile pictures, thumbnails, online stores, and other designs where the subject needs to appear without its original background.

Is an image background remover useful for product photos?

Yes. Removing a background can make product images easier to place on websites, online stores, advertisements, catalogs, and social media designs.
